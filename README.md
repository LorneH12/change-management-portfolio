## American Express | January 2026–Present

Enterprise change management supported by training, stakeholder communication, content development, cross-team partnership, and analytics.

### Flagship: Jira Data Center and Rally to Jira Cloud

- 60,000 impacted users; seven personas; three-month program.
- 59 live sessions across six global time zones: US West, US East, Manila, New Delhi, Sydney, and London.
- Worked as part of a team. Personally produced training videos and PowerPoint decks, hosted live sessions, and owned analytics and communication of findings to stakeholders and executives.
- Supported resource management, shaped messaging, and partnered with communications and cross-functional teams.
